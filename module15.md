# MODULE 15 — REMOTE MEDICAL INTERVIEW MASTERY

> Part of: **AfyaDesk Remote Medical Careers Course**

## Learning Objectives

- Prepare tech + environment for flawless video interviews
- Answer common, behavioral, and Medical VA scenario questions with STAR
- Discuss salary, gaps, and "Why hire you?" confidently
- Demonstrate professionalism + scope safety live

## 1. Video Setup (Interview Is a Telehealth Demo)

Background: plain wall/blur, clinic-smart dress, headset, eye-level camera, light on face, backup hotspot + phone ready. Join 10 min early, name "Faith N – Medical VA", mic muted until start. Have: CV, job post, 3 stories, 2 questions for them, pen + tracker sample to screen-share. Close tabs, silence phone, inform household.

## 2. Common Questions + Strong Answers

- Tell me about yourself (90 sec): Present (MVA-trained, KRCHN) → Past proof (30 patients/day, 9% no-show) → Future (want long-term with [Clinic], ET overlap).
- Why remote? "I thrive on systems + written communication – in clinic I ran recall lists with 95% follow-up. Remote lets me focus on coordination + documentation I excel at."
- Why hire you? 3 pillars: clinical literacy + admin accuracy + reliability (backup power/internet + EOD reports) + 60-sec proof.
- Gaps: honest + learning: "2023 gap caring for family – kept skills via HMIS volunteer + this AfyaDesk course + 5 simulations."
- Salary: research ($5-10/hr entry Kenya remote), give range + emphasize value + trial: "Based on 3 yrs + scheduling/EHR + ET nights, $7-9/hr contractor, open to 2-week paid trial to prove fit. How is pay structured here?"

## 3. Medical VA Scenario Questions

- Angry patient waiting 40 min? → HEARD + 2 options + document (Module 5).
- Double-booked? → Own, fix with consent, prevent with checklist.
- Portal chest pain? → Urgent flag + ER advice + document, no diagnosis.
- Doctor's calendar chaos? → Audit, buffers, waitlist, daily prep email.
- PHI in AI? → "Never in public AI – only BAA-approved, otherwise manual in EHR."
- Night ET shift? → "Yes, 10pm-6am EAT setup tested: fibre + hotspot + power bank + quiet room – did 2-week simulation."

## 4. STAR Method

**S**ituation + **T**ask + **A**ction + **R**esult (numbers) + Learning. Example: "Clinic no-shows 18% (S), I owned reminders (T), built 48h SMS + 24h call list + waitlist fill (A), 9% in 6 weeks, +12 visits/week (R)." Prepare 6 stories: difficult patient, error fixed, tight deadline, tech fail, team conflict, process improved.

## 5. Mock Interview Rubric (AfyaDesk Standard)

Scored 10%: punctual/tech (10), English clarity (20), clinical-admin knowledge (20), scenarios/scope (20), reliability/timezone plan (15), questions + close (15). Pass 80%+. Record, self-score, redo weak answers.

Strong close: "Thank you – excited about [Clinic]'s [specialty]. Based on what you shared, my scheduling + referral follow-up fits your backlog. Next steps? I can share test task + references by tomorrow 10am ET."

## Practical Assignment

Complete 30-min mock (peer/trainer): setup photo + 10 Q&A recording + 2 scenarios + salary answer + follow-up thank-you email within 24h.

## Common Mistakes

- Pajamas + noisy background, "I can do anything"
- Badmouthing past employer, guessing clinical answer instead of escalating
- No questions for them – always ask: "What does success in 30-60-90 days look like? Biggest admin pain now?"

## Self-Check Quiz

1. STAR your no-show win in 4 lines.
2. Safe answer to dosage question in interview?
3. Salary range script?
4. 3 tech checks pre-interview?
5. Best 2 questions to ask employer?

## Key Takeaways

Prove you are safe, clear, and dependable on camera – that's the job preview.
