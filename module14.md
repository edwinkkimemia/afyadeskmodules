## 2. Reading Job Descriptions Like a Recruiter (Must vs Nice, Tools, Shift, Pay)

Example decode: “US mental health seeks FT Medical VA 9am–5pm ET (4pm–12am EAT), 2 yrs scheduling, SimplePractice + eligibility + 50 WPM required, prior auth preferred, HIPAA-aware, $7–$9/hr contractor via Deel, 2-week paid trial, growth to lead.” Must-haves (knockout if missing): FT 9–5 ET overlap (can you work midnight Kenya 5 nights? backup plan? health?), 2 yrs scheduling (prove 25/day + no-show cut, not just “helped”), SimplePractice (sandbox + Loom or transferable Athena + fast-learner plan 5 days), eligibility (portal + benefit quote script + verification ID habit), 50 WPM (keybr.com screenshot 55+). Nice-to-haves (apply if 70%+ core + show plan): prior auth (packet mock + CoverMyMeds tour + “approve in 3 days” story from Module 9). Pay $7–$9 contractor (no benefits, Deel fees, KRA tax — net maths + trial pay confirmed in writing). Trial 2 weeks (they’ve been burned — be flawless: EOD daily, zero zone misses, phishing-proof, escalation <15 min). Growth to lead (document SOP + train next hire signal in cover).

Note tools (Athena? Teams? Asana? — mirror exactly, don’t write “EHR experience” vague), shift (ET nights = 10pm–6am EAT California — can family/sleep support? propose 4pm–12am ET East vs PT West preference), pay type (hourly contractor with tracker vs monthly retainer vs salary with EOR benefits — confirm pay day/method/fees/overtime/leave), contract length + termination (14-day? 30-day? non-compete scope?), location (US-only due licensing? worldwide with EAT overlap okay?). If <70% must-haves or night shift impossible long-term, save energy — apply where win rate highest. Mirror phrases verbatim in CV (“patient portal triage” not “inbox handling” if post says portal) — ATS + human keyword match doubles shortlist.

Red-flag posts (skip + report): $30/hr no experience + Telegram only, “no camera interview,” fee for training/laptop, Gmail with no website/LinkedIn staff, check-forwarding, ID/passport upfront. Legit posts have company domain, staff LinkedIn, website + reviews, video interview, written contract with pay day/method. Score scam 0–5 before applying — 2+ flags = verify deep or skip (Section 5).

## 3. ATS + Tailoring + Cover + Cold That Win Shortlists

ATS parses text, not design. Same titles/skills verbatim, simple bullets with numbers/tools/zones, standard headings, PDF+DOCX, filename `Faith-MedicalVA-Acme-2026.pdf`, no tables/images/headers/footers. Tailor 15 min: headline + summary first 2 lines + top 4 bullets mirrored to post keywords + quantified (“Scheduled 30/day across 2 providers, 97% confirmation, SimplePractice + Teams, 4pm–12am EAT / 9am–5pm ET”). Quantify every claim — “cut no-shows 18→9% via Sunday SMS + waitlist fill (tracker).”

Cover 3 paras 120 words: Hook (“Admire Acme’s evening access for working parents in Ohio — 3-provider mental health with telehealth focus aligns with my intake + eligibility background”), Fit (“Confirmed 25/day with 97% + eligibility verified day-before with IDs + prior-auth packet approved 3 days in simulation (portfolio) + 55 WPM + HIPAA-aware + ET overlap with backup”), Close (“Available 15-min intro Tue–Thu 4–7pm EAT / 9am–12pm ET — portfolio + 60-sec video linked — test task welcome. Thank you for considering — Faith + phone”). No “To whom it may concern / Dear Sir” — find hiring manager (LinkedIn, hunter.io) “Hi Ms. Rivera.”

Cold to small practices (highest reply 10–15% vs 2% job boards): subject “Medical VA — Scheduling + Referrals — ET overlap — 2-min intro (Nairobi)” + 120-word pitch + 60-sec Loom + portfolio link + test-task offer + follow-up Day 7 once. “Hi Dr. Patel, noticed Oak Family (3 providers, Ohio) mentions long phone waits — I help similar practices cut no-shows 18→9% + close referrals 92% <14d with trackers/EODs (portfolio). Available 3–11pm EAT / 8am–4pm ET with backup. Open to 15-min intro + 1-hr test task (5 confirms + 1 referral mock)? Portfolio + video linked. Thank you — Faith.” 20 tailored colds/week >100 spray. Track opens (Mailtrack) + replies + interviews, iterate subject/hook weekly.

## 5. Avoiding Scams: Critical Survival Skill for Kenyan Jobseekers

Scammers prey on “US job” dreams + M-PESA fluency + urgency. Red flags (2+ = stop + verify deep): Telegram/WhatsApp-only interview (no video, no domain), fee for training/laptop/background (“Send 5k M-PESA to...” — legit employers pay you, never reverse), Gmail/Yahoo only with no website/LinkedIn company + staff, $5,000/month no experience + luxury car photos, check to cash + forward balance (money laundering — prison risk), ID/passport/bank upfront before video + contract, no video call ever (“camera broken” forever), pressure “decide in 1 hour or lose offer,” poor grammar + logo stretch + free website (Wix with no staff). Real US employers never pay via personal M-PESA to Kenya normally — Wise/Deel/Payoneer/wire with contract + invoice + tax docs.

Verification checklist before sharing data/working free: video call with camera (both sides, screen-share office/ID badge redacted, tour EHR sandbox mock), company domain email (not Gmail — verify SPF via hunter.io + reply from domain), LinkedIn company page + 5+ staff with history + website + Glassdoor/Indeed reviews + Crunchbase if startup, written contract PDF with rate/currency/pay day/method/fees/hours/zone/overtime/equipment/BAA/termination/metrics/signatures (not WhatsApp promises), pay method confirmed (Deel/Wise business, pay day 1st/15th, who pays fees, overtime), test task ≤1 hr paid or credited (never 8-hr free week), no upfront payment + no ID/bank till contract signed + minimum data (last-4 + redacted till BAA). Call official number from website (not number in message) to confirm hiring. Ask AfyaDesk + community “Anyone worked with X? Proof of payment?” Search “[Company] + scam/review/complaint.” Trust gut + data — walk away from pressure, keep evidence, report to platform + AfyaDesk + ODPC if data taken + Safaricom for M-PESA fraud line.

If scammed: stop sending money/data/work immediately, screenshot all chats/receipts/numbers, report M-PESA reversal (*234#) + police OB + platform + domain registrar, change passwords + MFA, freeze CRB if ID shared + monitor, debrief with AfyaDesk for lessons + support (shame delays reporting — report fast to protect others). Prevention beats recovery — 2-minute verification + never-pay-to-work rule saves careers.

## 6. Application Formula That Compounds: Research → Match → Tailor → Apply → Follow Up → Interview → Improve

**Research (10 min/post):** company (specialty, size, reviews, hiring manager name via LinkedIn/hunter.io, recent news), role (must vs nice, tools, shift, pay, trial, growth), competition (applicants count, posting age — <7 days + <30 applicants = high win). Save to tracker with legit score.

**Match (2 min):** % fit (70%+ core + zone + pay viable? — yes/no + gap plan). If referral needed + you lack + no time to mock-learn in 7 days, skip to higher-fit post — energy is finite.

**Tailor (15 min Section 3):** title/summary/4 bullets mirrored + cover hook-fit-close + filename versioned + portfolio link with 2 relevant samples (scheduling post → EOD + tracker; billing post → auth packet + denial appeal).

**Apply (5 min):** submit before 9am ET (top of inbox), ATS-friendly PDF+DOCX where required, Upwork proposal 150 words + Loom + 2 samples (not 10 attachments), cold email Tue–Thu 8am ET (open rates highest). Log date + version.

**Follow Up Day 5–7 once with value (not “any update?” nag):** “Hi Ms. Rivera, following up on Medical VA application 12 May — still very interested in evening access mission, available 4pm–12am EAT / 9am–5pm ET with backup. Recent win: cleared 15 referrals in 3 days in simulation with 92% closure (tracker linked). Happy to do 1-hr test task (5 confirms + 1 referral mock) this week. Thank you — Faith + phone + portfolio.” If no reply Day 14, close with grace (“Closing loop — remain interested if needs reopen, portfolio updated monthly — thank you!”) + keep networking (comment on their posts monthly). Track conversion: 20 apps → 3 replies → 1 interview normal Months 1–2; 20 → 5 → 2 Months 3+ with video + referrals. Review Fridays: which subject/hook/bullet got replies? A/B test one variable weekly (subject with zone vs without, Loom vs no Loom, 120 vs 80-word cover).

**Interview → Improve loop (Module 15 prep):** every interview debrief 15 min (questions asked, STAR stories used, salary discussed, tech test, what stumbled, what to fix before next). Update CV/bank/SOP with lessons. Ask permission “May I follow up in 2 weeks for feedback to improve?” — 20% reply with gold. No ghosting bitterness — thank + move to next 20.
