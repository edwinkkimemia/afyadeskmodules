## 1. Freelance Model for Medical Support: Niche Retainer Business That Scales From Nairobi

Start freelance, not to stay “gig” forever, but to build proof + reviews + retainers that become stable income. Path for Kenyan clinicians: Months 0–3: 1–2 clients 10–20 hrs/week each @ $6–$9/hr hourly (flexible, learn US systems, build 2×5-star reviews with metrics) → Months 4–8: raise to $8–$11 + convert to monthly retainer (e.g., 80 hrs/month @ $800 = $10/hr effective, paid 1st via Deel, hours tracked but not haggled) → Months 9–18: 2 retainer clients 60–80 hrs each @ $10–$12 ($1,200–$1,920/month) or 1 full-time 160 hrs @ $10 ($1,600) + referral pipeline 3rd client for cover. Niche beats general: “Telehealth scheduling + referral coordination for US mental health practices (SimplePractice, PHQ-9/GAD-7, eligibility, 4pm–12am EAT overlap)” commands $9–$12, while “General VA — data entry, anything” stuck at $4–$5 with 60 applicants/post. Why? Practices pay premium for specialist who cuts no-shows 18→9% and closes referrals 92% <14d without training, not generalist who learns on their dime.

Positioning statement for profiles: “[Niche] for [Client type] with [Proof metric] + [Tools] + [Zone/backup] + [Availability].” Example: “Telehealth scheduling + referral closure for US family/mental health (3-provider) — cut no-shows 18→9% + 92% closure <14d in AfyaDesk simulations with trackers/EODs — SimplePractice/Athena + Teams/Zoom/Slack + 4pm–12am EAT / 9am–5pm ET with fibre+hotspot+power bank — available 20 hrs/week retainer with 90-day review to $11. Portfolio + 60-sec video. Test task welcome.” This specificity filters cheap $3/hr seekers and attracts $8+ clients who value clinical literacy + reliability.

Business maths to memorize: 20 hrs/week × $8 = $640/month gross per client minus Deel 5% + Wise $5 + 10% KRA provision + 20% savings = ~$470 net — need 2 clients for KSh 90k+ net sustainable (2×20 hrs = 40 hrs/week = full-time without single-point failure). Retainer maths: 80 hrs/month @ $10 = $800 fixed (not hourly haggle) = $9.20/hr effective after fees vs hourly $8 with tracker friction — retainer rewards efficiency (AI saves 5 hrs/week = your profit, not fewer billable). Price retainer at 90% of hourly × hours (80×$10=800) not 100% to give client predictability + you stability. Review every 90 days tied to metrics (no-show <8%, closure >85%, SLA 100%, zero breaches) + new skills (prior auth, billing support, RPM) for $1–$2 raise + expanded scope with revised SOW addendum, not verbal “more work same pay” creep.

## 2. Finding Clients and Winning Proposals (Hook-Proof-Plan-CTA + Loom in 150 Words)

**Where to hunt (20/week target, 45 min search + 45 min tailoring + 30 min network routine from Module 14):**

Upwork: filter Healthcare + US/UK payment verified + $6+/hr + 10–50 hires + <15 proposals + Last 7 days + client rating 4.5+ + verified. Read top 10 winning profiles (Jasmin, Philippines, $12/hr mental health) — note titles, overview first lines, portfolio samples, review language (reliability + no-show cut), then position Kenyan clinical edge (KRCHN vs general VA) + EAT overlap for East. Avoid $3/hr “data entry 100 rows $5” sweatshops — bid 20% higher with proof beats 50 cheap bids. Save + track legit score 0–5 (flags Section 5 Module 14) before bidding.

LinkedIn: Jobs (Remote + Medical VA/Patient Coordinator/Scheduler) + Services marketplace (create “Medical Scheduling + Referrals” service with packages) + founder hunt (mental health clinic owners, dental practice managers — hunter.io email + personalized note vs blank connect). Follow 20 target clinics + comment thoughtful 3×/week (Module 13 activity) before pitching — warm beats cold 3×.

Practice websites direct (highest win 10–15% vs 2% boards): Google “family medicine Ohio ‘careers’ OR ‘join us’ OR ‘contact’” + “mental health Austin Texas ‘intake coordinator’” + “dental Seattle ‘front desk remote’” — find 1–5 doctor sites with “We’re hiring” or “Long phone waits” reviews (Google 4.2 stars “phone never answered” = your hook). Get founder/manager email (Hunter, RocketReach, website contact + phone call “May I email hiring manager about support?”). VA agencies subcontracting for experience (HelloRache, CareChoice — apply as contractor, 6-month bridge, learn BAA + QA + US systems, then direct). Track 20 targets/week with Company | Pain (reviews/posts) | Decision-maker | Email | Approach date | Follow-up.

**Proposal 150 words that beat 50 applicants (template + why each part wins):**

Hook (2 lines, clinic-specific pain, not generic “I’m VA”): “Noticed Oak Family’s recent reviews mention 20-min phone holds and open referrals — I help 3-provider US practices cut no-shows 18→9% and close referrals 92% <14d so doctors regain 10 hrs/week.” Personalization proves research, not spray — managers read 10 seconds, hook decides continue/delete.

Proof (3 lines, 1 metric + tool + zone/backup): “Confirmed 25/day across 2 providers with SimplePractice + Teams, 97% with dual-zone + SMS, eligibility verified day-before with IDs — available 4pm–12am EAT / 9am–5pm ET with fibre + hotspot + power bank + EOD daily (portfolio + 60-sec video linked).” Numbers + tools + zone + backup + proof link = low-risk hire.

Plan (3 lines, Week 1 audit + quick win, no vague “quick learner”): “Week 1: audit calendars + trackers + inboxes + SOPs with you, fix 3 quick wins (Sunday SMS + waitlist fill 30 min + referral receipt confirm 24h), share 1-page audit + SOP diff. No disruption — shadow then own.” Plan shows initiative + systems, not “tell me what to do.”

CTA (2 lines, 15-min + test task, de-risk): “Open to 15-min intro Tue–Thu 4–7pm EAT / 9am–12pm ET + 1-hr paid test (5 confirms + 1 referral mock with tracker/EOD) to prove fit before commitment? Portfolio + video below — thank you, Faith +1...” Test offer triples replies — try before buy.

Attach: 60-sec Loom (0–5 smile + headset/blur, 5–15 KRCHN 4 yrs + 30/day, 15–35 scheduling/referrals/EHR/60 WPM/HIPAA, 35–45 EAT overlap + backup, 45–55 portfolio + test-task CTA — 10 takes till natural <65 sec), 1-page sample tracker (mock, no PHI, conditional formatting, 92% closure proof), EOD sample (numbers + blockers). Price: hourly $7–$9 entry to start (not $12 without reviews), retainer 80 hrs/$800 Month 2 post-trial with 90-day review to $10–$11 tied to metrics (Section 1). Never free 8-hr week (“exposure”) — 1-hr paid test max, then paid trial 2 weeks via Deel. Send Tue–Thu 8am ET (top of inbox US morning) + Mailtrack open log + follow-up Day 7 once with value (template mock) + Day 14 graceful close if silent. Log proposal variant + open + reply + interview to A/B test subject/hook/Loom weekly — 15% cold reply is excellent, iterate to get there.

## 3. Contracts, Invoicing, and Getting Paid: Wise/Deel/Payoneer + KRA Without Surprises

Contract 1-pager PDF via HelloSign/DocuSign (never WhatsApp promises) with 8 clauses you propose (shows professionalism, not bossy):

1. **Scope (tasks + hours + zone + exclusions):** “20 hrs/week Mon–Fri 4pm–12am EAT / 9am–5pm ET: scheduling/confirms/reminders/waitlist, referral coordination 92% <14d, inbox triage <4h with SBAR escalation, telehealth prep/links/summaries, EHR admin notes + tasks + uploads, weekly report. Excludes: clinical advice, coding, personal errands, website. Change requests >2 hrs/week = SOW addendum + revised quote.” Prevents scope creep (“just add billing + Instagram”) that kills $8/hr to $3 effective.

2. **Rate + pay day/method/fees:** “$8/hr, bi-weekly via Deel (client pays Deel 5% fee, you receive net via Wise, pay day 1st/15th, invoice due Net 7, late fee 1.5%/month after 15 days, work logged via Toggl).” Confirm gross vs net, who pays Deel/Wise $5 wire, currency (USD), overtime 1.5× after 40h/week, public holiday pay. Get pay day in contract, not “end of month-ish” — late pay is top freelance stress.

3. **Term + termination + notice:** “Initial 1 month, auto-renew monthly, 14-day written notice either side, 30-day for retainer, no non-compete beyond 30 days + 50-mile (unreasonable 12-month global non-compete = decline).” Protects both: client not locked with poor fit, you not dropped overnight without income bridge. Keep 1-month emergency fund from Month 1 (20% savings).

4. **Confidentiality + DPA/HIPAA/BAA:** “BAA signed via employer/Deel, Kenya DPA 2019 + HIPAA-aware handling: minimum necessary, approved channels only, no PHI in public AI/personal, encrypted devices, 2FA, breach report 1 hour, training yearly, audit logs available. Return/delete PHI within 7 days of termination per retention, certificate of destruction.” Attach BAA + training cert. Breach = termination + liability — take seriously.

5. **IP + work product:** “Work product (SOPs, trackers, templates created on paid time) licensed to client for use, you retain generic methods/portfolio rights with de-identified samples (no PHI) for future proof.” Prevents “you can’t show anything forever” lock.

6. **Communication SLA + coverage:** “Response <15 min Slack in shift, <4h email/portal, EOD daily 5 lines, weekly report Fri 4pm ET, handover written if off, backup coverage peer [Name] for 1 day/month with client approval + BAA, PTO 5 days after 6 months with 14-day notice + coverage plan.” Defines urgency (no 2am “urgent” unless ER-level per SOP).

7. **Equipment + stipend:** “Own laptop/headset/internet/power per Module 16, client provides EHR logins + BAA cloud + $0 stipend Month 1, review $200 home-office stipend Month 3 on meeting 3 metrics — invoices + photos.” Ask after offer, not before (greedy first impression).

8. **Review + raise + reference:** “30-day trial review (3 metrics: no-show <10%, closure >80%, SLA 100% → $8→$9), 90-day review (5 metrics + SOP training 1 hire → $9→$11 + lead). Reference letter + testimonial on success + Upwork/LinkedIn endorsement.” Ties pay to value, not begging.

Get accountant (KRA-accredited, KSh 3–5k/quarter) from Month 1: foreign income taxable (declare via iTax, keep invoices/contracts/bank statements 5 years, provision 10% + 20% savings + NSSF/SHA voluntary via e-Citizen for continuity), invoice numbering `INV-2026-05-Faith-001` with dates/hours (Clockify CSV export: date/task/hours/notes) + rate + total USD + KES at CBK rate that day + due date + Pay to (Wise USD details: name, ACH routing, account) + late fee note + retention “Keep 5 years.” Send Net 7, follow-up Day 8 polite (“Gentle bump — INV-001 $640 due yesterday via Deel — link resend? Thank you!”) + Day 15 with fee per contract, pause work Day 21 per notice (not ghost). Never personal M-PESA from US employer normally (scam flag Section 5 Module 14) — Wise/Deel business with contract + invoice + tax docs is proof.

## 4. Boundaries and Retention: Systems That Keep Clients for Years, Not Weeks

**Onboarding that prevents 80% of friction (intake form + 30-60-90):** Intake (tools access list with logins/MFA/BAA, SOPs version + tracker links, escalation contacts (who for clinical urgent vs billing vs tech, phone + Slack), comms hours + SLA + #urgent rules + handover format, patient panel size + specialties + payer mix + top pains, compliance (BAA/DPA training + NEVER list + breach card)). 30-60-90 plan shared Week 1: Days 1–30 shadow + audit calendars/trackers/inboxes + 3 quick wins (Sunday SMS, waitlist 30 min, receipt confirm) + EOD daily + weekly report with audit + SOP diff; Days 31–60 own 80% with owner/dates + close 20 referrals + cut no-show 2% + 1 SOP trained; Days 61–90 lead own metrics (closure >85%, SLA 100%, zero breaches) + train peer 1 hr + propose automation saving 5 hrs/week with privacy gates + 90-day review with raiseask backed by numbers + testimonials. Client signs 30-60-90 — expectations aligned, no “I thought you’d do website too.”

**Weekly/monthly rhythms that make you irreplaceable:** Sunday inbox zero plan posted 3 lines, daily EOD 5 lines with numbers + blockers + tomorrow top 3, Friday weekly report 1-pager (booked/seen/no-show/fill %, referrals closed %, auth time, A/R >30, CSAT + 2 actions + risks) presented 5 min on huddle, monthly value report 1-page for owners (kept visits + revenue math + 5-star quotes mentioning you + hours saved via AI + next month 2 improvements). Owners forward value reports to partners — your raise case writes itself.

**Boundaries with “yes-with-options” language (no angry no, no doormat yes):** Define hours (Mon–Fri 4pm–12am EAT / 9am–5pm ET, response <15 min in-hours, <4h email, off 12–4pm EAT unless STAT per SOP) + SLA in contract + auto-reply outside hours (“Received outside 9–5 ET — reviewed 9am ET next business day. For ER go 911 now. — Faith analogue?” actually patient auto-reply via practice, yours to team: “Offline till 4pm EAT — urgent via @here #urgent + phone + EHR flag — handover in EOD”). Change requests >2 hrs/week: “I can add Sunday 4-hr telehealth block at 1.5× ($12/hr) or move Fri admin to Sun same hours — which works + shall I send SOW addendum for scope/hours/rate/term?” No clinical/personal errands ever: “I’m not qualified for dosage/diagnosis — flagged to Dr. X pool urgent 2pm with safety net + booked 9am review — shall I arrange?” + log. Scope creep script: “Happy to help with billing calls — currently 20 hrs scheduling/referrals. Adding 10 hrs billing support = 30 hrs total — SOW addendum $8×20 + $10×10 = $260/week or keep 20 hrs and prioritize? Advise + I’ll draft addendum.” Professional, not apologetic — clients respect priced boundaries more than free “yes” that burns you out Week 6 and quits.

**Referrals that fill pipeline without cold pain:** Ask after win, not ask: “Glad referrals now 100% closed with 8-day avg (was 22) — who else in your network struggles with open referrals/phone waits? Intro gets them free 1-hr audit + you 2 hrs credit. May I send 2-line intro you can forward? Thank you for trust!” + collect testimonials (ask permission + specific: “Can I quote ‘Faith cut no-shows 18→9% and never missed despite rains — backup flawless’ with name/title? LinkedIn + portfolio?”) + star ratings on Upwork/LinkedIn + case study 1-pager (challenge → actions → metrics → quote) with de-identified data. They compound: 1 happy client → 2 referrals → 1 new retainer in 60 days without cold. Ask every 90 days post-value-report, not weekly nag.

## Common Mistakes That Keep Freelancers at $3/hr Forever

Undercharging $3/hr to win, never raising (client anchors low — start $7–$9 + 30/90 review), no contract (scope creep to billing + website + Instagram + personal errands for same $8), “just add...” yes without addendum (burnout Week 6 quit), mixing client PHI across drives/logins (breach + both clients fire — separate BAA folders + accounts + wipe between), free 8-hr test weeks (“exposure” — 1-hr paid max), no intake/30-60-90 (misalignment → “I thought you’d answer 24/7”), no EOD/weekly (invisibility → “what do you do?” → not renewed), no referral ask (cold forever), personal M-PESA from “US client” (scam — Wise/Deel business only). Pin rate + scope + boundary + intake checklists above desk for first 6 months.
