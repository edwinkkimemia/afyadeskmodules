# MODULE 19 — FREELANCING & INTERNATIONAL CLIENTS

> Part of: **AfyaDesk Remote Medical Careers Course**

## Learning Objectives

- Launch freelance VA services legally from Kenya
- Find clients, write winning proposals, onboard professionally
- Contract, invoice, get paid (Wise/Payoneer/Deel), handle tax basics
- Set boundaries + earn referrals for long-term retainers

## 1. Freelance Model for Medical Support

Start: 1-2 clients 10-20 hrs/week @ $6-9/hr → prove → raise to $10-12 + monthly retainer (e.g., 80 hrs/month $800). Niche: "Telehealth scheduling + referral coordination for US mental health practices" beats "general VA." Profiles on Upwork + LinkedIn + direct outreach to 20 practices/week.

## 2. Finding Clients + Proposals

Where: Upwork (filter Healthcare + US, <10 proposals, verify payment), LinkedIn, small practice websites (psych, dental, PT – founder email), VA agencies as subcontractor for experience.

Proposal (150 words): Hook (their pain – "missed calls + open referrals"), Proof (1 metric + tool + timezone), Plan (Week1 audit + quick win), CTA (15-min call + test task). Attach 60-sec Loom + 1-page sample tracker (mock, no PHI). Price: hourly to start, retainer after 1 month. Never work free >1-hr test.

## 3. Contracts, Invoicing, Getting Paid

Contract must have: scope (tasks + hours + timezone), rate + pay day (e.g., $8/hr, bi-weekly via Wise), term + 14-day termination, confidentiality + DPA/HIPAA clause, IP, communication SLA, late fee. Use HelloSign +/nm templates reviewed.

Pay: Wise, Payoneer, Deel, direct wire – confirm who pays fees, KRA tax: declare foreign income, keep invoices + contracts 5 yrs, consult accountant. Invoice template: Invoice # | Dates/hours (Clockify export) | Rate | Total USD + KES | Due date | Payment details.

## 4. Boundaries + Retention

Onboarding: intake form (tools access, SOPs, escalation contacts, comms hours) + 30-60-90 plan. Weekly update email + monthly value report (no-shows cut, referrals closed). Boundaries: defined hours + response SLA, change requests = revised quote, no clinical tasks, no personal errands. Say no professionally: "I can add Sunday coverage at 1.5x or move Friday tasks – which works?"

Referrals: ask after win: "Glad referrals are now 100% closed – who else in your network struggles with this? Intro gets you 2 hrs free." Collect testimonials + star ratings – they compound.

## Practical Assignment

Create Upwork + LinkedIn service page, 3 proposals for real posts, 1 contract + 1 invoice + onboarding checklist.

## Common Mistakes

- Undercharging $3/hr forever, no contract, scope creep ("just add billing + website")
- Mixing client PHI across drives – separate folders + logins per client
