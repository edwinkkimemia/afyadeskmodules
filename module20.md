## 1. Career Readiness Assessment (8 Gates, 80%+ to Pass)

1. **CV:** ATS + quantified, remote title, timezone – reviewed vs Module 13 rubric.
2. **LinkedIn:** photo/headline/About/skills + activity + custom URL.
3. **Terminology test (50 Q):** prefixes, abbreviations, safe documentation, scope.
4. **Communication:** email + call recording scored for warmth/clarity/zone/scope.
5. **VA practical:** live day – 15 confirms, inbox triage, referral tracker, EOD report, zero critical errors.
6. **Computer skills:** 50 WPM typing, Sheets filter + calendar dual-zone + Teams/Zoom share.
7. **Remote-readiness:** backup internet/power photo + speed tests + quiet workspace + night-shift availability plan.
8. **Mock interview:** Module 15 rubric, scenario + salary.

Remediation: 1 retake per gate with coaching plan. Portfolio holds scores – employers see proof, not just certificate.

## 2. Capstone Project – You Are Hired as MVA

Scenario (from main.md): private practice, 1 doctor, 20 patients.

In 48 hours submit:
1. Calendar managed (no conflicts, buffers, zones)
2. 10 appointments scheduled + confirmations sent
3. 3 patient emails answered (1 angry, 1 clinical-edge with safe escalation)
4. 1 cancellation filled from waitlist
5. 1 referral processed end-to-end with tracker
6. 1 admin record updated + filed correctly
7. Follow-up list (labs + recalls with dates/owners)
8. Difficult interaction script + HEARD log
9. Clinical escalation (chest pain message) flagged + documented
10. EOD report (Day1 + Day2) + time log

Graded on: accuracy (40), communication (25), privacy/scope (20), timeliness/systems (15). Distinction 90%+ featured to Talent Pool.

## 3. AfyaDesk Talent Profile – Your Portfolio

Include: professional name/photo, summary, qualification, experience, skills, certs (AfyaDesk cert # + verification link), remote skills, specialties, software, English level, availability + timezone overlap, CV PDF, assessment scores, certificate, interview status. Example summary: "Medical VA – 95% VA practical, 92% terminology, ET nights available, backup fibre+hotspot – ready for scheduling/referral roles."

Keep updated monthly with wins + testimonials. This becomes your remote CV link.
