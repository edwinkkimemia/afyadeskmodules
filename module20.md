## 1. Career Readiness Assessment: 8 Gates, 80%+ to Pass, 1 Retake With Coaching (Proof > Promises)

Gates mirror hiring funnel — fail one = specific coaching, not shame. All 80%+ = AfyaDesk Certificate with unique verification ID + profile featured.

**Gate 1 — CV (ATS + quantified + zone):** Reviewed vs Module 13 rubric (7-sec test + Notepad + keyword mirror 8/10 + metric every bullet + no photo + 1–2 pages + PDF/DOCX + referees with permission). Submission: CV PDF/DOCX + 1 target post + match table. Common fail: duties without numbers (“responsible”) — fix with Action-Task-Result + tool + zone.

Fail: “Any job please help” + no headline keywords — fix with Module 13 framework + 60-sec video linked.

**Gate 3 — Terminology (50 Q, 60 min, 80% to pass):** prefixes/roots/suffixes 15, abbreviations safe vs Do-Not-Use 10, anatomy/disease 10, meds/labs/imaging 10, scope boundary 5 with SBAR + safe notes. Retake: Anki 20/day + read-back drills 10 min/day + mock audio 5-min transcription. Score shows clinical literacy without scope violation.

**Gate 4 — Communication (email + call 15 min, warm/clarity/zone/scope):** 1 patient email (confirmation with dual zone + link + prep + Reply YES), 1 SBAR escalation (chest pain portal 2:10pm + what told patient + need), 1 call recording 3 min (verify 2 IDs + summarize + 2 options + read-back NATO + written twin). Scored warmth 20 + clarity 20 + zone 20 + scope 20 + close 20. Fail: “Sawa/ASAP/no zone/diagnosis” — redo Module 12 gym + re-record.

**Gate 5 — VA Practical (live 8-hour day, 15 confirms, inbox, referrals, EOD):** Simulated day (Module 2 + 3 + 6 + 9): 15 confirms with SMS/call dual-zone + waitlist fill, 5 inbox (1 urgent chest pain <15 min phone + flag + ER + SBAR, 2 today, 2 week), 2 referrals Day 3-7-14 logs + receipt + note retrieval mock, EOD + handover. Zero critical errors (wrong chart, missing zone, PHI in personal, diagnosis) auto-fail. Proves you can do job day 1.

**Gate 6 — Computer (50 WPM + Sheets + Calendar + Teams/Zoom):** keybr.com 50+ WPM with 95%+ accuracy + Sheets filter/conditional/red + Calendar dual-zone with 5 events + secondary + reminders + colours + Teams/Zoom share-screen + chat + waiting room demo. Fail: 35 WPM + one zone — drill 15 min/day + 14-day sprint Module 11 Appendix A.

**Gate 7 — Remote-readiness (backup audit, 10/10 photo):** 1-pager backup (Internet primary speed screenshots 3 days + hotspot 10GB + bank/UPS + alternate + power + device encryption + separate account + lock + manager + 2FA) + workspace day/night photos + noise MP3 + drill recording fibre cut 30 min with flag/SMS/phone/log + health plan for nights (sleep/blackout/family/meals/movement). Fail: “Fibre enough” no backup — fix Module 16 + re-audit.

**Gate 8 — Mock interview (30 min, 100-point rubric Module 15, 80%+):** 10 Q&A + 2 scenarios (angry HEARD + chest pain urgent) + STAR with metrics + salary range + trial + 2 smart questions + close + thank-you 4h + screen-share tracker/EOD. Scored tech 10 + English 20 + knowledge 20 + scenarios 20 + reliability 15 + close 15. Zero scope violation (any dosage/diagnosis/reassurance) auto-fail. Proves preview = trial-ready.

## 2. Capstone Project: 48-Hour “You Are Hired” for 20-Patient Private Practice (Proving, Not Passing)

Scenario (from main.md): You are Medical VA for 1-doctor private practice (Dr. Lee, family medicine, 20 patients, US East). In 48 hours submit 10 deliverables proving Day 1 competence (accuracy 40 + communication 25 + privacy/scope 20 + timeliness/systems 15 = 100, pass 80%+, distinction 90%+ featured):

1. **Calendar managed (buffers, zones, no conflicts):** 20 patients across Mon–Tue with visit types (new 40, FU 20, urgent 15 buffer, video + in-person), buffers 10 min/hr, blocked admin 12–1pm, colours (green confirmed/tech-checked, yellow pending forms/consent, red risk), waitlist 3, huddle email Mon 7am with numbers + gaps + flags. No double-books, 100% dual-zone titles/descriptions, reminders 24h+1h.

2. **10 appointments scheduled + confirmations sent:** new + FU + urgent + telehealth mix, each with reason, DOB, 2 phones, insurance, referral, language, video/link or address, zone, consent, backup. SMS + email twins within 5 min, log, tracker.

3. **3 patient emails answered (1 angry, 1 clinical-edge, 1 routine):** angry 40-min wait → HEARD + 2 options + SMS + manager flag + log + follow-up next day; clinical “Should I double dose? dizzy” → boundary + escalation to pool urgent 2pm with safety net (avoid driving, stay hydrated, seek care for fainting/chest) + booked 9am review + documented no advice; routine “Confirm Tue?” → warm confirmation with month + dual zone + link + prep + Reply YES. All <150 words, subject-tagged, signature hours both zones, no diagnosis/reassurance, logged to chart.

4. **1 cancellation filled from waitlist:** Thu 2pm ET cancelled → filled in 30 min priority urgent→FIFO with consent logged, both parties SMS, calendar + tracker updated, no-show reason logged + barrier fix (Sunday SMS for forgot).

5. **1 referral closed-loop with tracker:** Patel→Cardiology Lee for LBP foot drop ? disc herniation, packet (order + notes + X-ray + failed PT + auth form via Availity mock) + receipt confirm 24h + patient booked Tue 2pm ET / 9pm EAT + attended mock + note retrieved + routed to PCP pool with 2-line summary (“Cardiology 12 May: MRI ordered, f/u 4 weeks, please review”) + patient notified + tracker closed <14d.

6. **1 admin record updated + filed correctly:** address/phone/insurance correction with addendum timestamp + verification ID, doc uploaded named `2026-05-12_Smith-J_Referral_Cardiology-Lee.pdf` tagged to encounter, no cloning, header re-checked, task closed.

7. **Follow-up list (labs + recalls with dates/owners):** 5 labs (HbA1c, CBC, lipids, TSH, X-ray) with ordered/done/reviewed/relayed/follow-up booked columns + Day 3-7-14 chase dates + owners, 3 recalls (diabetes 90d, mammogram 365d, vaccines) with due + SMS batch dates + barriers, tickler reviewed Mon huddle.

8. **Difficult interaction script + HEARD log:** 3-min angry call transcript + HEARD steps + 2 options + log (date/time/issue/action/outcome/follow-up) + CSAT ask next day. No “calm down/that’s policy.”

9. **Clinical escalation flagged + documented:** portal chest pain 2:10pm ET 58M HTN tight chest 20 min → reply “call 911/go to ER now, alerting team, stay on line, location/callback?” + phone pool + EHR urgent flag + SBAR (S/B/A/R + what told patient + need) + timestamp + 30-min follow-up, no diagnosis, safety net documented. Auto-fail if reassured.

10. **EOD reports Day 1 + Day 2 + time logs:** Day 1: 12 confirms, 1 referral sent, inbox 15→3, 2 gaps filled, auth pending, blockers; Day 2: 10 confirms, 1 referral closed, labs chased, no-show rebooked, tomorrow prepped; Hours via Toggl per task, total 16, handover written.
