## 1. What Remote Employers Scan in 7 Seconds (And Why Most Kenyan CVs Fail)

Recruiter opens 100 PDFs for “Medical VA.” ATS parses first. Human skims 7 seconds: title match? (“Medical Virtual Assistant / Healthcare Admin / Patient Coordinator” — not “Enrolled Nurse seeking greener pastures”), 3 core skills visible? (scheduling, EHR/data entry, patient communication with tools: SimplePractice/Athena, Teams/Zoom/Slack, Workspace/365), metrics? (25/day, 98%, no-show 18→9%, 60 WPM), timezone + availability? (“Nairobi EAT, overlap 3–11pm EAT / 8am–4pm ET, backup hotspot/power”), English clarity? (no “sawa/revert/ASAP,” month names + zones). Fail any = delete. No photo on CV (US/UK bias law — photo triggers bias filter), 1–2 pages max, PDF + DOCX, simple fonts (Calibri/Arial 10–11), standard headings (Summary, Skills, Experience, Education), no tables/images/headers/footers/text boxes (ATS can’t read).

Kenyan CVs fail by: 4 pages with KCPE/primary school, “Referees available on request” wasting half page, duties listed (“Responsible for ward”) without numbers/tools/zones, Gmail “sweetie254@” unprofessional, no LinkedIn, no metrics, no remote keywords (portal, prior auth, HIPAA-aware, EAT/ET). Fix in this module — your CV becomes remote proof, not local history.

## 5. Tailoring in 15 Minutes Per Job (Spray Dies, Tailored Wins Interviews)

Spraying same CV to 100 posts = 0 replies. Tailoring top 3 sections per post = 20 apps → 3 replies → 1 interview normal. Steps 15 min:

1. **Extract keywords (3 min):** Highlight titles, skills, tools, shift, metrics in post (“SimplePractice, eligibility, 50 WPM, 9am–5pm ET, prior auth preferred, HIPAA”). Paste into table Job Keyword | My Proof | CV Line #.
2. **Mirror title + summary (4 min):** Change title line + first 2 summary lines to post titles/skills verbatim naturally (“Patient Care Coordinator with portal triage + SimplePractice + eligibility...”). Keep truthful — mirror language, don’t invent experience.
3. **Reorder + rewrite top 4 bullets (6 min):** Move most relevant role/experience top, rewrite 4 bullets with post verbs + numbers + tools + zone (“Triaged 30 portal messages/day in SimplePractice with <4h SLA + SBAR escalation, 0 urgent misses — available 4pm–12am EAT / 9am–5pm ET”). Quantify every claim.
4. **Check ATS + send (2 min):** Notepad paste test, filename `Faith-MedicalVA-Acme-2026.pdf`, cover note 120 words (hook with clinic name/specialty + 2 proofs + availability + test-task offer + portfolio link), tracker entry (company/role/link/keywords/date/follow-up Day 5–7). Follow up once with value (“Recent win: cleared 15 referrals 3 days in simulation — happy to do test task”).

Example tailoring: post “Mental health seeks FT VA 9am–5pm ET, SimplePractice, eligibility, 50 WPM, prior auth nice, HIPAA” → title “Medical VA | Mental Health Intake, SimplePractice, Eligibility & Prior Auth Support,” summary first line “... mental health intake 20/day in SimplePractice with PHQ-9/GAD-7 + eligibility + 55 WPM...,” bullets reordered (intake, eligibility, auth, inbox), cover hook (“Admire Acme’s evening access for working parents — I cover 4pm–12am EAT...”). Same person, 15-min mirror, 3× replies.
