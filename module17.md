# MODULE 17 — WORKING ACROSS TIME ZONES

> Part of: **AfyaDesk Remote Medical Careers Course**

## Learning Objectives

- Convert confidently between EAT, UTC, UK, US, Australia zones
- Account for daylight saving without missing appointments
- Schedule + communicate dual-zone correctly
- Manage night shifts healthily from Kenya

## 1. Foundations: UTC + EAT

UTC = global base. Nairobi EAT = UTC+3 year-round (no DST – advantage, you are stable). UK GMT (winter, UTC+0) / BST (summer, UTC+1, last Sun Mar–last Sun Oct). US ET: EST UTC-5 winter / EDT UTC-4 summer; CT +1h behind ET, MT +2, PT +3. Australia: Sydney AEST UTC+10 / AEDT UTC+11 (Oct–Apr – opposite to US).

Key conversions (Nairobi 3pm EAT):
- London winter 12pm, summer 1pm
- New York winter 7am, summer 8am
- California winter 4am, summer 5am
- Sydney winter 10pm, summer 11pm

| Nairobi EAT | London | New York | California | Sydney* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9am | 6am/7am | 1am/2am | 10pm/11pm prev day | 4pm/5pm |

*First = winter UK/US, second = summer; Sydney flips – always verify.

Tools: WorldTimeBuddy, Google Calendar secondary zone (Nairobi + New York), Every Time Zone. Phone world clock with 4 cities.

## 3. Scheduling Rules

Always write both: "Tue 14 May, 10:30 AM ET / 5:30 PM EAT – video." Create calendar event in employer's zone (usually ET), enable secondary zone view. Confirm DST change weeks: "US springs forward 9 Mar 2026 – your 3pm EAT shift becomes 8am ET (was 7am)." Double-check recurring meetings post-DST.

## 4. Managing Night Shifts from Kenya

US day = Kenya evening/night (e.g., 9am-5pm ET = 4pm-12am EAT winter, manageable; California 9am-5pm PT = 7pm-3am EAT – harder). Sleep: blackout curtains, 7-8h daytime sleep, consistent schedule, no caffeine after 2am, 20-min nap pre-shift if needed. Health: light meals, water, 5-min stretch hourly, annual checkup. Negotiate: 4x10 vs 5x8, weekend rotation limits, overtime pay.
