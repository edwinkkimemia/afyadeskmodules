## 1. Appointment Management as Revenue Engine (Your Daily Profit Lever)

Every kept visit = $120–$250 primary care, $300+ specialty in US. Every no-show = $0 + wasted doctor time + longer backlog. At 20 visits/day, cutting no-shows 16%→8% saves ~1.6 visits/day × 22 days ≈ 35 visits/month ≈ $5,250/month — 4× your salary. This maths wins interviews: “I protect revenue, not just book.”

System: template confirmations 48h + 24h + same-day with dual-zone + link/address + prep + callback + reschedule link; waitlist FIFO + urgent priority filled in 30 min of cancellation with consent logged; huddle list daily (tomorrow confirmed %, gaps with waitlist names, VIP/urgent flags, tech risks, interpreter needs); no-show protocol call 15 min + SMS + email + reason log + rebook 7 days + barrier fix (transport → video, forgot → double reminders, cost → payment plan info, fear → nurse call).

No-show rate = no-shows ÷ booked (exclude cancelled-rescheduled-kept). Confirmation rate = confirmed ÷ booked. Fill rate = filled cancellations ÷ cancellations. Same-day fill >70% excellent. Track weekly by provider, reason, and slot type — pattern tells fix: Monday 8am high forgot → Sunday evening reminder; elderly high tech → phone + test-call; new-patient high insurance confusion → pre-visit eligibility call.

Script fill: “Hi Ms. Wanjiku, a 10:30am tomorrow with Dr. Lee opened — you’re first on waitlist, shall I hold 2 hours? ... Held till 4pm EAT, SMS + email sent with 10:30 AM ET / 5:30 PM EAT + link + prep. Reply YES. Anything else?” Log consent time. Never pressure sick to unsuitable slot — offer two, document choice.

## 2. Patient Registration That Prevents Denials (One Digit = $250 Denial)

Registration errors cause 30–40% of US denials: wrong member ID digit, expired coverage, missing referral, name mismatch (married vs ID), DOB flip (US MM/DD trap). Your checklist prevents all:

Full legal name (as on insurance card, verify spelling NATO), DOB (write “14 May 1980” not 05/14), sex + pronouns as chart, phone + backup + SMS consent, email for portal/link, address (billing vs mailing), emergency contact + relationship + phone, photo ID front/back, insurance front/back photos (member ID, group, payer phone, PCP on card, effective dates), PCP + referring provider, allergies bolded, language/interpreter + communication needs, consent (treatment + telehealth + financial + privacy + photo if async) with date/method/version, pharmacy (name, address, fax), employer (for workers’ comp if relevant).

Eligibility verification day-before via Availity/payer portal + phone if flagged: active? effective? in-network for this provider/location? deductible ($500 total, met $200, owes $300 before coverage)? copay ($30 specialist vs $15 PCP)? coinsurance (20% after deductible)? referral/auth required? visit limit (PT 20/year, used 12)? Document verification ID, date-time-zone, representative name if called, screenshot to EHR. If inactive/needs referral, call patient today: “Your Aetna shows inactive from 01 May — alternative coverage? I can hold slot 48h while you confirm with HR/payer at +1-... Shall I also check self-pay $120 option?” Never let patient arrive to surprise $250 bill — warning today saves 1-star + collection cost.

Read-back + photo verify: “Member ID AET884112, Group 4521, DOB 14 May 1980 — correct? Card photos legible, front/back uploaded, tagged to 14 May encounter.” Update EHR + tracker + huddle flag if high-risk (auth pending, referral missing). Kenyan HRIOs: translate NHIF/SHA habits (contribution status check) to US deductible logic — same diligence, new vocabulary.

## 4. Insurance, Claims Support, and Billing Support Without Coding (Support Role, Not Coder)

US basics in patient-friendly lines: copay (“fixed $30 you pay at visit, like consultation fee”), deductible (“$500 you pay first each year before insurance pays — you’ve met $200, $300 left”), coinsurance (“after deductible, you pay 20%, insurance 80%”), out-of-pocket max (“most you pay yearly, then 100% covered”), prior auth (“insurer permission before MRI/procedure — we submit notes, reply 3–5 days”), referral (“PCP permission to see specialist — we obtain”), EOB (“statement, not bill — shows what insurance paid/denied and why”).

VA tasks: eligibility check (Section 2), benefit quote with disclaimer (“Estimate $30 copay + $120 toward deductible based on Aetna portal today — final per EOB, questions? Call payer +1-...”), clean-claim support (ensure clinician-selected ICD/CPT + modifiers + auth number + NPI + place-of-service attached, no blanks, attachment legible), claim status tracking (Submitted → Acknowledged → Paid/Denied/Partial — check Availity/Change weekly, log), denial triage (CO-97 auth missing → attach auth + appeal with notes; CO-50 non-covered → verify ABN signed + offer self-pay plan; CO-16 referral missing → obtain + resubmit; PR patient responsibility → gentle billing call).

Billing call script: “Hi Mr. Smith, this is Mercy from Oak Clinic about 12 April visit balance $45 after Aetna paid $105 (EOB 28 April). I see no payment yet — would you like to clear via portal now (link SMS) or 2-part plan $22.50 today + 28 May? I can also check itemization + payment assistance if needed. What suits you?” Never threaten collections on first call — offer options + document promise + follow-up date. Escalate hardship to biller/manager with notes.

Never code diagnosis/procedure yourself unless CPC-certified and authorized. If clinician note says “chest pain” but coder needs specificity, query: “Dr. X, for billing, please clarify chest pain type/location/duration for coding — EHR shows ‘chest pain’ only. Shall I hold claim pending addendum?” Document query + answer. Your accuracy prevents fraud (upcoding) + denials (downcoding).

## 5. Prior Authorization: Packets That Approve in Days, Not Weeks

Needed for MRIs, CTs, procedures, surgeries, brand drugs, therapy, DME (wheelchairs), genetic tests. Payer asks: is it medically necessary + cheapest safe alternative tried? Your packet proves yes.

Packet checklist: order with ICD + CPT + urgency (routine 5–14d, urgent 72h, STAT 24h) + rendering provider NPI + place-of-service, clinic notes (HPI, exam, failed conservative: “PT 6 weeks, NSAIDs, no relief, VAS 7/10, foot drop noted”), relevant labs/imaging (X-ray + report, not just “pain”), prior treatments with dates/doses/duration, auth form in payer portal (Availity, CoverMyMeds, eviCore) fully filled (no blanks — blanks = auto-deny), patient contacts + preferred facility in-network, cover letter 3 lines (“45M LBP with foot drop, MRI L-spine requested, conservative failed, notes + X-ray attached, please expedite per deficit”).

Submit → record auth tracking # + portal screenshot + submission date-time-zone → follow up 48–72h (routine) / 24h (urgent) via portal + phone (reference #, rep name, ETA) → if denied, read reason (missing notes? wrong CPT? need peer-to-peer?) → supplement + appeal in 7 days or schedule peer-to-peer (you book doctor-to-doctor call, prepare one-pager, remind both, document outcome) → on approval log auth # + expiry + visits/units approved + facility + patient SMS booked → tickler for expiry (auth 60–90 days typical). Keep patient updated every 3 days (“Submitted 12 May, payer says 3–5 days — I check daily, SMS on decision, no action needed now — questions? Reply here”). Silence breeds “you forgot my MRI!” calls + 1-stars.

Example log: Smith J MRI L-spine | Aetna #PA-8841 submitted 12 May 3pm ET via Availity | Rep Lisa ETA 15 May | F/U 14 May — pending | Approved 15 May #AET-9912 exp 15 Aug 1 scan at Open MRI Main | Patient booked 20 May 2pm ET SMS sent | Tracker closed 15 May MK. Approval in 3 days vs 14 = patient loyalty + $1,500 scan revenue protected.

## 6. Follow-Up Ticklers and Weekly Admin Reporting That Earn Raises

Ticklers (never rely on memory): labs pending (ordered/done/reviewed/relayed/follow-up booked — open >7d red), referrals open (sent/receipt/booked/attended/note/closed — >14d red), recalls due (HbA1c 90d, mammogram 365d, vaccines — due this month list), auths expiring (expiry 30/14/7d alerts + re-auth trigger), balances >30d (promise dates + follow-up calls), tasks >7d ownerless. Review every Monday huddle 15 min: reds with owner + next action + date. No red without owner/date.

Weekly one-page report to doctor/manager (Friday 4pm ET): Booked/Seen/No-show/Cancel-fill % with trend vs last week + top reason + fix (“No-shows 12%→8% after Sunday SMS — continue”), Referrals sent/closed % + avg days + oldest open + action, Auths submitted/approved/denied/pending + avg days + peer-to-peers, Claims submitted/paid/denied + A/R >30/60/90 + top denial reason + appeal status, Inbox SLA (100% <4h?), CSAT/reviews (new 5-stars, complaints closed), Next week top 3 risks + 2 proposed actions + staffing needs. One page, numbers + arrows + 2 actions, link trackers. Managers forward this to owners — your visibility = value. Volunteer to present 5 min on huddle — leadership signal.

Documentation standard: every admin action timestamped both zones + verification ID + outcome + initials. “Eligibility verified Aetna portal 13 May 4pm EAT / 9am ET ID V12345 active, $30 copay, $300 deductible left, referral not needed — MK.” Audits, handovers, and denial appeals live on these lines.
